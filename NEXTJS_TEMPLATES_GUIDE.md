# 🚀 Next.js Templates Arsenal for Business Outreach

**Purpose**: Use these stunning, production-ready templates as "hooks" to show businesses what their website could look like. Match template to business type based on your website scanner results.

## 📊 How to Use This Guide

1. **Run your website scanner** to identify businesses needing redesigns (high Facelift Priority scores)
2. **Match business to template category** below (restaurant → restaurant templates, retail → e-commerce, service → landing page)
3. **Clone and customize** the template with their branding
4. **Show them the demo** as part of your outreach pitch

## 🎨 CUSTOMIZATION PRIORITIES

When customizing for outreach demos:

### Priority 1 (Must Change)
- ✅ Business name in header
- ✅ Hero headline
- ✅ Primary CTA button text
- ✅ Contact information

### Priority 2 (Should Change)
- ✅ Color scheme (use their current brand colors)
- ✅ Logo (if available)
- ✅ 2-3 service/product descriptions
- ✅ Social media links

### Priority 3 (Nice to Have)
- Photos (use stock or their existing photos)
- Full menu/product catalog
- Blog posts
- Team members

**Remember**: For outreach, you need just enough customization to show them "this could be YOUR site" - don't spend hours perfecting it!
